How a large language model is trained

The steps from raw text to a deployed assistant, and what each resource costs.

The training pipeline
  1. Collect dataWeb pages, books and code
  2. Clean and tokenizeRemove poor text, split into tokens
  3. PretrainPredict the next token on thousands of GPUs
  4. Fine-tuneLearn from example answers
  5. AlignHuman feedback (RLHF)
  6. EvaluateBenchmarks and safety tests
  7. DeployApps and APIs

Pretraining (highlighted) uses most of the compute and money.

More data, more parameters and more compute make models more accurate, which is why labs keep building larger clusters. Estimated compute cost grew about 17 times from GPT-3 to GPT-4, so very few organizations can train a frontier model. Fine-tuning and alignment use little compute, yet they turn a text predictor into an assistant people can use.
